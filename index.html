Privacy Policy

Effective 26 September 2026  ·  Last updated 26 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Block Blitz mobile game (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information. We built Block Blitz to be simple and respectful of your privacy: we do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (scores, coins, levels, stars, unlocked skins, power-ups and settings such as sound and music) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us, and we cannot see it. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset Progress, or by uninstalling the App.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the App may automatically collect and share with Google:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID (Android advertising identifier)Showing and measuring ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention
IP address (used to estimate general location, e.g. country)Ad delivery, legal compliance, fraud prevention
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Ad delivery and diagnostics
Ad interactions (ads viewed or tapped) and crash/performance diagnosticsMeasurement, analytics and fraud prevention

This data is processed by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • How Google uses data for advertising

Some ads are optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch them in exchange for an in-game reward (for example a free revive or bonus coins). Rewards are only in-game items with no real-world value.

3. Your choices and consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the App shows Google’s consent form, where you can accept or decline personalised ads. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy Options. If you decline, Google may still show non-personalised ads.
  • Personalised ads on Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • US state privacy laws: you may opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising using the options Google provides and your device’s ad settings.

4. Children

Block Blitz is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help address it.

5. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information and we do not have access to it. The only third party that receives data from the App is Google, as described in section 2, for advertising and consent management (Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform).

6. Security and retention

Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Google keeps it according to its own retention policies. Game progress stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the App.

7. Permissions

The App uses the Internet permission to load ads and the advertising ID permission for the purposes above. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when features change. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App means you accept the updated policy.
